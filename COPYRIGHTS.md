## Manufacturer identification colors

Seagate, Toshiba, Western Digital, HGST and Ultrastar names/marks belong to their
respective owners. Category bands use color references to identify manufacturers;
they do not indicate affiliation or reliability. No manufacturer logo artwork is
bundled. The pinned palette is Seagate green `#6EBE49`, Toshiba red `#CC0000`, and
WD blue `#005195`, including the model bands beneath each manufacturer.

References: [published Seagate brand guide](https://www.slideshare.net/slideshow/seagate-brand-g-uide/79342401),
[Toshiba color reference](https://www.brandcolorcode.com/toshiba), and the
[WD palette supplied during review](https://www.designpieces.com/palette/western-digital-color-palette-hex-and-rgb/).
The WD palette is dated 2015; it is used as the owner's familiar identification
blue, not represented as a newly issued corporate style guide. The official
[Seagate brand portal](https://branding.seagate.com/) requires access.
