## Thank you, Backblaze

This project is possible because **Backblaze collects, maintains and openly
publishes the Drive Stats dataset**. Backblaze is the original source of the
drive observations used here. We appreciate the sustained work involved in
making this evidence available to everyone.

- [Backblaze Drive Stats dataset, schema, downloads and usage rules](https://www.backblaze.com/cloud-storage/resources/hard-drive-test-data)

back to those original resources. Our interpretations do not represent
Backblaze's conclusions or explain its purchasing decisions.

## What is distributed here

Raw ZIPs, daily CSVs, private Parquet caches, drive serial numbers, reference XLSX
files and owner screenshots are not committed. The checked-in website data is
derived, aggregate analysis. Development fixtures are explicitly labeled samples.

Quarterly JSON includes source and coverage information. Website views link to
Backblaze prominently, and CSV downloads include data-source, source-URL, quarter,
data-kind and analysis-project fields so attribution travels with the results.
Sample exports identify illustrative data instead of attributing invented values
to Backblaze observations.

Keep the source, coverage, population selection and calculation versions with any
published visualization. Capacity represents the stated observed-drive population;
AFR and inferred departures use the documented definitions. The analysis adds
interpretation and presentation to Backblaze's data rather than claiming ownership
of the original observations.
